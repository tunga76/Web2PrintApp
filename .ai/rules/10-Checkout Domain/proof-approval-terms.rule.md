# Proof Approval and Legal Terms Rules (Web-To-Print)

## Core Principles

- **Customer Liability for Content:** In the web-to-print industry, the platform is not responsible for spelling mistakes, low-resolution warnings ignored by the user, or copyright violations in user-uploaded files.
- **Custom-Made Products Exemption:** Legally, products manufactured to the consumer's specific specifications or clearly personalized are exempt from standard e-commerce return/refund policies (Right of Withdrawal / Cayma Hakkı).

## Digital Proof Approval (Soft Proofing)

- **Explicit Consent:** During the checkout process (or right before adding to cart), the user MUST explicitly check a mandatory box indicating their approval of the digital proof.
  - *Example Text:* "I have reviewed my design. I confirm that all text, spelling, and layouts are correct. I accept responsibility for any low-resolution images used."
- **Audit Logging:** The system must record the exact timestamp, IP address, and the specific version ID of the design (`DesignId`) that the user approved when they checked the box. This serves as vital legal protection against chargebacks, refund requests, or reprint demands.

## Return Policy & Checkout Requirements

- **Checkout Checkbox:** Alongside the standard "I accept the Terms and Conditions", there must be an explicit acknowledgement of the personalized goods return policy before the payment button becomes active.
  - *Example Text:* "I understand that these are custom-printed products and cannot be returned or refunded unless there is a manufacturing defect."
- **Order Confirmation Email:** The post-checkout order confirmation email must reiterate these terms and provide a thumbnail or link to the approved digital proof as a receipt of exactly what was authorized.

## Copyright and Content Policy

- **Content Declaration:** Include a clause in the Terms of Service during checkout where the user declares they own the rights (or have permission) to print the uploaded logos, images, and text. The platform must explicitly reserve the right to cancel orders containing illegal, offensive, or copyright-infringing materials without penalty.
