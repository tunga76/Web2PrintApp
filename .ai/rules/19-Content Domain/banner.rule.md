# Banner & Promotion Placement Rules

## Core Principles

- **Dynamic Placements:** Banners are not just images; they are strategic assets. The system must support dynamic placements (e.g., "Homepage Hero", "Category Sidebar", "Cart Top").
- **Targeting:** Support displaying banners based on user context (e.g., showing a specific banner only to the `VIP` Customer Group, or based on the active Campaign).

## Scheduling & Metrics

- **Time-Bound:** Banners must support `StartDate` and `EndDate`. The frontend must automatically hide expired banners without requiring a code deployment.
- **Analytics:** Attach tracking parameters (UTM or internal tracking IDs) to banner links to measure Click-Through Rates (CTR) and conversion attribution.
