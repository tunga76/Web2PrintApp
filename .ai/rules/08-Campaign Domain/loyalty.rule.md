# Loyalty Program Rules

## Core Principles

- Customers earn points based on their purchases and can redeem points for discounts or rewards.
- The loyalty system must maintain a strict, auditable ledger of point transactions (Earned, Redeemed, Expired, Refunded).

## Earning Points

- Points are calculated based on the final paid amount (excluding shipping, taxes, and applied discounts).
- Points must be pending until the order return window passes, after which they become available.
- Support bonus point campaigns (e.g., 2X points on weekend).

## Redeeming Points

- Support conversion rate (e.g., 100 Points = $1).
- Allow users to pay partially or fully with points.
- Points used should be distributed proportionally across order items for accurate refund calculations.

## Expiration

- Points must have an expiration policy (e.g., expire after 1 year of inactivity).
- Notify customers 30 days before point expiration.

## Refunds

- If an order is refunded, earned points must be deducted.
- If an order paid with points is refunded, points must be returned to the customer's balance.

## Tiers

- Support customer tiers (e.g., Bronze, Silver, Gold) based on lifetime value or points earned in a year.
- Different tiers can have different earning multipliers.
