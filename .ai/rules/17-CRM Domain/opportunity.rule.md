# Opportunity (Deal) Rules

## Core Principles

- **B2B Sales Funnel:** An Opportunity represents a potential high-value sale, usually involving negotiation (typical in B2B or wholesale).
- **Pipelines:** Opportunities move through a customized Sales Pipeline (e.g., `Prospecting`, `Needs Analysis`, `Proposal Sent`, `Negotiation`, `Closed Won`, `Closed Lost`).

## Financials

- Every Opportunity must have an `EstimatedValue` and a `Probability` (%) of closing. This allows sales managers to forecast future revenue accurately.
- When an Opportunity reaches `Closed Won`, it should seamlessly transition into the Order Domain (often via a finalized Quotation).
