# Customer Service Rules

## Core Principles

- **Omnichannel Support:** Customer Service tools must aggregate communications from multiple channels (Email, Live Chat, Phone, Social Media) into a single unified interface.
- **Actionability:** Agents must be able to perform actions (issue refunds, cancel orders, update addresses) directly from the customer service view without switching to a different administrative panel.

## Metrics & SLAs

- Support defining Service Level Agreements (SLAs) for response and resolution times (e.g., "First response within 2 hours").
- Track agent performance metrics (Average Handling Time, Customer Satisfaction Score - CSAT) based on ticket resolutions.
