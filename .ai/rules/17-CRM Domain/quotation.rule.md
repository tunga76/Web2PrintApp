# Quotation (Quote) Rules

## Core Principles

- **Formal Offer:** A Quotation is a formal, time-bound offer of products/services at a specific price given to a customer or prospect.
- **Snapshot Pricing:** Like Orders, Quotes must take a snapshot of the prices at the time they are generated. If a catalog price changes tomorrow, an active Quote must retain its offered price.

## Lifecycle

- Quotes have validity dates (e.g., "Valid for 30 days"). Once expired, they cannot be accepted without re-evaluation by a sales rep.
- **Custom / Non-Catalog Items:** In the printing industry, Quotes often contain entirely bespoke items that do not exist in the public Catalog (e.g., a custom dimension or a special paper type). The system must allow Sales Reps to add free-text "Custom Line Items" with arbitrary pricing to a Quote.
- **Design Attachment:** A Quote should allow the Sales Rep or the Customer to attach the necessary Print Files/Designs during the negotiation phase.
- **Conversion to Checkout/Order:** If a customer accepts a Quote online, the system must allow "One-Click" conversion of the Quote directly into a pre-filled Shopping Cart or a `PendingPayment` Order. All negotiated prices, custom descriptions, and attached Design Files must transfer flawlessly.
