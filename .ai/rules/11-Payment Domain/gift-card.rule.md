# Gift Card Rules

## Core Principles

- **Monetary Equivalent:** A Gift Card is treated as a form of payment, not a traditional discount coupon. It holds a specific monetary balance.
- **Unique Identifiers:** Gift Cards have unique, highly secure, non-sequential codes to prevent guessing attacks.

## Usage

- Similar to Wallets, Gift Cards support partial redemptions. The remaining balance stays on the card.
- Multiple Gift Cards can typically be applied to a single order.

## Lifecycle

- **Issuance:** Can be purchased (digital or physical) or issued by customer service.
- **Expiration:** Track expiration dates based on local consumer protection laws (which often prohibit or limit gift card expirations).
