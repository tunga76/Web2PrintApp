# Refund Workflow

Order
↓
Refund Request
↓
Eligibility Check
↓
Payment Verification
↓
Provider Refund
↓
Stock Decision
↓
Invoice Adjustment
↓
Customer Notification
↓
Refund Completed

---

Rules

Refund amount cannot exceed paid amount.

Partial refunds supported.

Multiple refunds supported.

Refunds must be audited.

All refund operations require reason codes.

Examples:

CustomerRequest
DamagedProduct
LostShipment
Fraud
PriceAdjustment
